### EXP-2026-09-16-mc-crsp

Written and committed before any CRSP scoring run of this spec. Kristen delegated the Stage 0 and
Stage 4 calls on 2026-09-16 ("approve, I trust your judgment; the objective is alpha or the highest ROI").

**Hypothesis.** The frozen momentum_concentrated spec (top-20 S&P 500 members by 12-1 momentum,
inverse-vol weights, 4 weekly tranches, 20% vol target capped at 2x, de-lever to 1x under the 200-day
SMA) earns a premium over a leverage-matched S&P 500 position, because 12-1 winners keep drifting
(underreaction and flow chasing), and that premium is not an artifact of the survivor-only yfinance
universe it was built on.

**Layer touched.** A (economic), against the registered baseline: CRSP S&P 500 total return scaled
to the spec's average gross exposure, with financing charged on the levered part at the T-bill rate.

**Alpha type tag.** market

**Data.** CRSP daily stock file (`crsp.dsf`), point-in-time S&P 500 membership (`crsp.dsp500list`),
daily delisting returns (`crsp.dsedelist`, Shumway −30% for a performance delisting with no return),
CRSP S&P 500 index total return (`crsp.dsp500.vwretd`) as the SPY stand-in for the 200-day gate,
T-bill and factors from `ff.factors_daily` / `ff.factors_monthly`. Closes are total-return indices
rebuilt from CRSP returns, keyed by PERMNO. Harness unchanged: `research/hunt2026/harness.py`
(10 bps/side stocks, one-day lag, gross cap 2.0).

**Evaluators.**
- **A, primary, never seen:** 1992-01-01 → 2004-12-31. The spec's parameters were fit on data from
  2005 onward (`panel_2005.parquet`), so this era is out of sample for every choice in the spec.
- **B, secondary, survivorship size:** 2005-01-01 → 2024-12-31 on CRSP with delistings, against the
  same window with only securities that survive to 2024-12-31. The gap is the bias the yfinance
  build carried. B informs, it does not decide.

**Expected result.** Net Sharpe 0.6–0.9 in A, alpha to MKT+UMD near zero (the spec is a concentrated
momentum long leg, so most of its return should load on UMD and the market), and a survivorship
gap in B of 1–3 percentage points per year.

**Alternative result.** If the mechanism is absent or was a survivor artifact: net Sharpe in A below
the leverage-matched index, and B's survivor-only run beating the delisting-inclusive run by more than
3 pp/yr.

**Kill condition (decidable from harness output, one trial, no tuning):** retire the book from live
trading if, in evaluator A, ANY of:
1. net Sharpe < 0.5 (the repo's standing kill threshold), or
2. annualized net return, after financing on gross above 1.0x at the T-bill rate, is below the
   leverage-matched S&P 500 baseline, or
3. monthly alpha versus the four factors MKT, SMB, HML, UMD has a Newey-West t-stat below 2.0
   *and* criterion 2 also fails.
Keep it live if none trips. **Stop-iterating rule:** the spec is frozen. No parameter or construction
change is tried on CRSP under this experiment; a failure retires it, it does not get rescued.

**n_trials.** 1 (added to the reconstruction trial ledger).

**Adaptive-loop flag.** The spec was selected in hunt2026 after its holdout was read several times;
evaluator A is the first data it has never touched.

**Kill criteria in A:** (1) Sharpe 1.13 ≥ 0.5, pass. (2) CAGR 24.2% > baseline 10.9%, pass.
(3) alpha t 1.53 < 2 but criterion 2 passes, so not tripped. **Verdict: KEEP live.**

**What it means.** On data it never saw, the spec beat a leverage-matched S&P by 13 pp/yr, most of it
explained by market and momentum loadings (4-factor alpha +5.3%, not significant). In the era its
parameters were fit on, it trailed the index: post-2009 momentum has been weak. Survivorship bias in
the yfinance build was small and in the opposite direction from expected (−0.5 pp/yr): a survivor-only
universe drops acquired winners as well as failures. The live book is a momentum-factor position, not
an independent alpha source, and should be read against UMD.
